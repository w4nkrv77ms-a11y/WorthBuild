PRIVACY

Privacy at IdeaWorth

IdeaWorth is designed to collect only the information needed to provide the service, respond to requests, preserve project selections and improve the customer experience.

Information you provide

Information may include your name, email address, project preferences and messages you send to support. Payment card details are not collected on the current checkout page.

How information is used

We use submitted information to provide requested services, communicate with customers, maintain project workspaces and improve the platform.

Third-party services

When commercial payment, analytics or other external services are connected, their own privacy terms will also apply. IdeaWorth will not claim a connection that is not active.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support channel shown on the website.
