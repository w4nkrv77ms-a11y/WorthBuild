REFUND POLICY

IdeaWorth refund policy

IdeaWorth sells digital business-planning products and project systems. Because digital materials can be accessed immediately after purchase, refund eligibility depends on the circumstances of the request and the applicable checkout terms shown at purchase.

Requesting a refund

Contact IdeaWorth support promptly with the order email, purchase reference and a short description of the issue. We will review the request and respond according to the applicable purchase terms.

Duplicate or technical issues

If you were charged more than once for the same purchase, or a technical problem prevents you from receiving the purchased material, contact support so the issue can be investigated.

Third-party payment processing

Payments are processed through Paddle when the Paddle checkout is active. Paddle's checkout and applicable terms may also govern the transaction.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated as IdeaWorth's products and fulfillment process develop. The version applicable to a purchase is the one presented at checkout.
